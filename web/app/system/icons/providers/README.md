# Sign-in marks

These assets identify sign-in providers. Their owners retain their trademarks;
they are not Relumea identity assets or evidence of endorsement.

- `google.png`: [Google Identity branding](https://developers.google.com/identity/branding-guidelines), downloaded from `https://developers.google.com/static/identity/images/g-logo.png` and scaled to 40 pixels wide for the 20-pixel control. Preserve the original colors and proportions.
- `github.svg`: [GitHub logo toolkit](https://brand.github.com/foundations/logo), `GitHub_Invertocat_Black.svg` from `https://brand.github.com/GitHub_Logos.zip`. Render in black or white against the page theme.
- `gitlab.svg`: [GitLab press kit](https://about.gitlab.com/press/press-kit/), downloaded from `https://about.gitlab.com/images/press/gitlab-logo-500-rgb.svg`. Preserve the original colors and proportions.

`ProviderIcon.tsx` uses the Relumea user and shield icons for password and company
OpenID Connect. Every icon is decorative and needs a visible button label.
